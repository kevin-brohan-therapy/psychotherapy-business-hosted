Welcome to Threshold

Whether you're coming to therapy because of a specific difficulty, or with a broader sense of feeling stuck, overwhelmed or not fully yourself, therapy can provide a space to understand what is happening and begin moving towards meaningful change.

I'm Kevin Brohan, a psychotherapy trainee working with individuals and couples online and face-to-face in Manchester and Stockport.

My Ethos

Therapy offers a confidential and collaborative relationship in which there is time to explore thoughts, feelings and experiences more deeply than is often possible elsewhere. Some people come with a clear goal, while others simply know that something doesn't feel right. Both are valid places to begin.

My approach is shaped by my training, ongoing professional development and regular clinical supervision. Rather than offering quick solutions, my aim is to help you understand yourself, your relationships and the patterns that shape your life, working together towards greater freedom, vitality and choice.

You might recognise some of these experiences

People come to therapy for many different reasons. You may already know what you want to work on, or you may simply have a sense that something isn't quite right. Some of the questions below may resonate with your experience.

Relationships
  • Do your relationships follow repeating patterns?
  • Do you keep finding yourself entrenched in negative feelings in relationships?
  • Are you questioning your primary relationship?
  • Would you like to work on your relationship with your partner?
  • Are you unable to achieve intimate relationships?
  • Are you struggling to process feelings from a toxic relationship?
  • Do you worry that you might be bringing toxicity into your relationships?
Thoughts, Feelings and Behaviour
  • Are you struggling to cope?
  • Are you struggling to stop worrying?
  • Do you struggle to control your behaviour?
  • Do you experience sudden anger, fear or sadness?
  • Do you struggle to contain your feelings?
  • Does something about your life feel intolerable?
  • Are you struggling to cope with grief or illness?
Self-Understanding
  • Do you wish you felt differently about situations?
  • Do you struggle with tension between how you are and how you feel you should be?
  • Do you try hard, but never seem to succeed?
  • Are you always in a rush? Are things ever good enough? Are you constantly let down by other people or letting other people down?
  • Do you find yourself living old feelings?
  • Do you find yourself putting other people first?
  • Do you find yourself trying to hold things together, yet feel that no one really recognises you?
  • Are you troubled by thoughts, dreams or fantasies (sexual or non-sexual)?
  • Do you find yourself being pulled back into difficult experiences from your past?
